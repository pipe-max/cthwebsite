80 years, a great celebration

Celebration

We are preparing with great excitement for our highly anticipated year-end show, "The World We Build – 80 Years," a very special celebration honoring our school's 80-year history. It will be a meaningful opportunity to celebrate our journey and identity while showcasing the talents of our students and teachers.

Tickets are now available, and we invite all our families to secure their seats and join us for this wonderful celebration. Purchase your tickets through CTH.INGRESA.CO and be part of an unforgettable evening.

For more information: Phidias Platform
We look forward to sharing this very special moment with you!
