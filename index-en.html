Who we are

A community where diversity is respected and celebrated.

Our community is grounded in the principles of Jewish culture, pluralism, and respect for differences. Today, we welcome students and families of different faiths, enriching our community through diversity and mutual respect.

Our educational model, Horaá Mutemet — education for diversity — respects each student's learning pace and nurtures principled, curious, and independent individuals.
