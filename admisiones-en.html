Your family's next chapter starts here.

At Theodoro Herzl School, children begin their educational journey in K3, provided they are 3 years old by July 31. We guide you through every step of the admissions process.

How it works

One grade for every age and stage.

The table below shows the age requirement for each level. Following the Admissions Committee's evaluations and observations, the school may determine the appropriate grade placement for each accepted student.

Our academic program follows an international curriculum aligned with Colombian educational standards.

Have questions about the process?

Our admissions team is here to guide you through every step. Email us at admisiones@theodoro.edu.co or call (+57) 604 322 0180.
