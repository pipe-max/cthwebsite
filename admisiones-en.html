Your family's next chapter starts here.

At Theodoro Herzl School, children begin school in Kindergarten 3, turning 3 years old by July 31. We walk with you through every step of the process.

How it works

One grade for every age and stage.

The table below shows the required age for each level. Based on the admissions committee's evaluations and observations, and if accepted, the school may decide the grade the student enters.

Our curriculum is organized according to the international syllabus, validated against the Colombian curriculum.

Have questions about the process?

Our admissions team is here to guide you at every step. Write to us at admisiones@theodoro.edu.co or call (+57) 604 322 0180.
